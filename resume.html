Research

  1. Sep 2023 — present

    Human-Machine Teaming Laboratory

    Oregon State, with Julie Adams. I estimate, during a mission, whether a person and several robots are still an effective team: the person’s state, what each robot can still do, and whether communication is holding. I design and run the human-subject studies, including wildland fire, and I built the real-time physiological processing, the machine-learning models, and the IMPRINT Pro workload models.

  2. Aug 2021 — May 2023

    Georgia Tech, with Harish Ravichandar. Learned trait preferences for heterogeneous task allocation, and generated StarCraft II demonstrations to test a coalition-formation algorithm based on concurrent multi-task bandits.
