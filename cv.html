Research

Human-Machine Teaming Laboratory, Oregon State, 2023–present. I did not build the remote-pilot interface. I design and run the human-subject studies on it, including wildland fire, for one pilot supervising many uncrewed aircraft beyond visual line of sight. I built the real-time physiological processing, the machine-learning models, and the IMPRINT Pro workload models.

The published model moved predicted pilot workload from 77.8 to 91.8 as the delivery fleet grew. Unexpected events moved it further. Cognitive and visual demand, not aircraft count, drove the change (HFES 2025). The wildfire study is in review at Human Factors.

STAR Lab, Georgia Tech, 2021–2023. First-author trait-preference learning for heterogeneous task allocation. Generated StarCraft II demonstrations to test a coalition-formation algorithm.

Intelligent Motion Lab, Illinois, 2020–2021, with Kris Hauser. Built Jarvis, the manager for the TRINA nursing robot, and the robot’s spatial and temporal storage.
